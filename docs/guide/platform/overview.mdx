funcd is one server binary, `funcd`, controlled with one CLI, `funcdctl`. A call, an event or a deploy enters funcd.
funcd then runs the Functions' code, keeps their state and answers.

<PlatformDiagram />

## The platform

- [Architecture](architecture/index.md): the control plane, the data plane and the storage layer.
- [Workloads](workloads/index.md): Functions, Apps, Sites and [Workflows](workloads/workflow.md), their
  [artifacts](workloads/artifacts.md), the [runtime](workloads/runtime.md) that runs them, and their
  [configuration](workloads/configuration.md), ConfigMaps and Secrets.
- [Services](services/index.mdx): KV, Buckets, SQL databases and Lakes, the engines under them, Secrets, and
  [Events](services/events.md), the EventSources and Sensors that start workloads.
- [Security](security-and-networking/security.md): the enforcement points and the authorizer.
- [Networking](security-and-networking/networking.md): how a call reaches a worker.
- [Limits](limits-and-quotas/limits.md): CPU, memory, volumes and disk space.
- [Quotas](limits-and-quotas/quotas.md): how many stores a namespace holds, and how much each store holds.
- [Observability and monitoring](observability.md): the logs, metrics and traces that funcd records, and where to
  read them.
- [Disaster recovery](disaster-recovery.md): the backups, the restores, and the way back after a host loss.
